### What I do

I work on developer platforms at Maersk, building APIs and abstractions that let application teams focus on their services instead of the infrastructure underneath them.

We use a service mesh under the hood, but application teams don't need to know that. They interact with platform APIs; the platform takes care of the plumbing. No mesh configuration tutorials, no routing-policy archaeology, no asking application developers to become infrastructure experts.

The goal is simple: make the complex stuff disappear behind a good developer experience.

I'm particularly interested in platform engineering, distributed systems, and the art of making powerful infrastructure feel boringly simple.
